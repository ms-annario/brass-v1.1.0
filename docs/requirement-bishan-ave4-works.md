**Requested by:** Land Transport Authority, Network Operations
**Date issued:** 03/11/2026
**Effective period:** 01/12/2026 22:00 to 14/12/2026 05:00 (temporary)

## 1. Summary
Water main replacement will fully close Bishan Avenue 4 between the Library (Dir 2) stop and the Bishan Community Club (Dir 2) stop. Bus services using this section must divert for the whole period.

## 2. Road closure
- **Road:** Bishan Avenue 4, between Library (Dir 2) (53097) and Bishan Community Club (Dir 2) (53148).
- **Closure:** both directions, full closure, 24 hours a day.
- **Reason:** water main replacement and road reinstatement.

## 3. Affected area
Works zone of about 190 m × 200 m along Bishan Avenue 4, as drawn in the attached file `bishan-ave4-works-zone.json`. No vehicles other than works vehicles inside the zone.
