# Service Introduction Notice SI-2026-0098

**Issued by:** Land Transport Authority, Bus Services
**Date issued:** 03/11/2026
**Effective from:** 01/12/2026 06:00 (ongoing)

## 1. Summary
A new bus service 12B will link Bishan Interchange with the Bishan Shopping Centre via Bishan Road and Bishan Street 62, to serve residents while Bishan Avenue 4 is closed.
