# Road Works Notice RW-2026-1187

**Issued by:** Land Transport Authority, Network Operations
**Date issued:** 03/11/2026
**Effective period:** 15/11/2026 06:00 to 22/11/2026 23:00 (temporary)

## 1. Summary
Resurfacing and drainage works will close part of Paya Lebar Street 94 in both directions. Buses will be diverted for the whole period.

## 2. Road closure
- **Road:** Paya Lebar Street 94, between the Sports Hub stop and the Lavender Stn stop.
- **Closure:** both directions, full closure, 24 hours a day.
- **Reason:** road resurfacing and drain replacement.

## 3. Affected area
Works zone covering roughly 300 m around Paya Lebar Street 94 and the Lavender Stn stop. Heavy vehicles are barred inside the zone.

## 4. Affected services
| Bus | Change |
|-----|--------|
| 886 | Divert between Sports Hub and Waterloo St. Skip Lavender Stn (82003). |
| 63  | No change. |
| 457 | No change. |

## 5. Stops
| Stop | Code | Status |
|------|------|--------|
| Lavender Stn | 82003 | Closed for the period. Use Sports Hub (82002) or Waterloo St (82004). |
| Sports Hub | 82002 | Open. Temporary terminus for the diverted section. |
| Waterloo St | 82004 | Open. Temporary pick-up for riders from Lavender Stn. |

## 6. Required map updates
1. Add a road closure on Paya Lebar Street 94 for the effective period.
2. Draw the affected area around the works zone.
3. Re-route bus 886 around the closure, keeping Sports Hub and Waterloo St as stops.
4. Mark Lavender Stn (82003) as not served for the period.
